Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Aurellix Pay ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our payment platform and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Identity information: name, date of birth, nationality, government-issued ID numbers
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Financial information: bank account details, payment card information, transaction history
  • Business information: company registration details, business licenses, beneficial ownership information

1.2 Technical Information

  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, transaction timestamps, API call logs
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and settling payment transactions
  • Identity verification and anti-money laundering (AML) compliance
  • Risk assessment and fraud prevention
  • Providing customer support and resolving disputes
  • Improving our services and developing new features
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Sending service-related notifications and updates

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, banking partners, and technology vendors essential to our operations
  • Regulatory Authorities: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you have given us explicit permission

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, network segmentation, and regular security audits to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable laws and regulations. Transaction records are typically retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with financial regulatory requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

7. International Data Transfers

As a global payment platform, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for platform functionality and analytics cookies to improve our services. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through our platform. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@aurellix.com
  • Telegram: @cs_aurellixpay

Aurellix Pay（以下简称"我们"）致力于保护您的隐私并确保您个人信息的安全。本隐私政策说明了我们在使用我们的支付平台及相关服务时如何收集、使用、披露和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 个人信息

  • 身份信息：姓名、出生日期、国籍、政府签发的证件号码
  • 联系信息：电子邮箱、电话号码、邮寄地址
  • 财务信息：银行账户详情、支付卡信息、交易记录
  • 商业信息：公司注册信息、营业执照、实际受益人信息

1.2 技术信息

  • 设备信息：IP 地址、浏览器类型、操作系统、设备标识符
  • 使用数据：访问页面、使用功能、交易时间戳、API 调用日志
  • Cookie 及类似的跟踪技术

2. 信息的使用方式

  • 处理和结算支付交易
  • 身份验证及反洗钱（AML）合规
  • 风险评估与欺诈防范
  • 提供客户支持与争议解决
  • 改进服务及开发新功能
  • 遵守法律与监管义务
  • 发送服务相关通知与更新

3. 信息共享与披露

我们不会出售您的个人信息。我们可能在以下情况下共享您的信息：

  • 服务提供商：对运营至关重要的支付处理商、银行合作伙伴和技术供应商
  • 监管机构：法律、法规或法律程序要求时
  • 业务转让：涉及合并、收购或资产出售时
  • 经您同意：您已明确授权许可时

4. 数据安全

我们采用行业标准安全措施，包括加密、访问控制、网络分段和定期安全审计，以保护您的信息免遭未经授权的访问、篡改、披露或销毁。

5. 数据保留

我们将根据本政策所述目的或适用法律法规的要求，在必要期限内保留个人信息。根据金融监管要求，交易记录通常至少保留 7 年。

6. 您的权利

根据您所在的司法管辖区，您可能享有以下权利：

  • 访问并获取您的个人数据副本
  • 请求更正不准确的信息
  • 请求删除您的个人数据（受法律义务约束）
  • 反对或限制特定处理活动
  • 数据可携带权
  • 随时撤回同意

7. 跨境数据传输

作为全球支付平台，您的信息可能会被传输到您居住国以外的国家并在其中进行处理。我们确保为此类传输提供适当的安全保障措施，包括标准合同条款和充分性认定。

8. Cookie 与跟踪技术

我们使用必要的 Cookie 来保障平台功能，并使用分析 Cookie 来改进服务。您可以通过浏览器设置管理 Cookie 偏好。

9. 未成年人隐私

我们的服务不面向 18 岁以下的个人。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

10. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。我们将通过电子邮件或平台通知您重大变更。在变更后继续使用我们的服务即视为接受更新后的政策。

11. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有疑问或希望行使您的权利，请联系我们：

  • 邮箱：privacy@aurellix.com
  • Telegram：@cs_aurellixpay
