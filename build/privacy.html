Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Aurellix Pay ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and corporate information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our global treasury, B2B collection/payout, OTC settlement, and acquiring services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Identity & KYB Information

  • Identity information: name, date of birth, nationality, government-issued ID numbers
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Financial information: bank account details, virtual account records, settlement history
  • Business information: company registration certificates, shareholding structures, beneficial ownership (UBO), and trade/logistics contracts

1.2 Technical Information

  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, transaction timestamps, API call logs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and settling global collections, FX conversions, OTC trades, and payouts
  • Identity verification (KYC/KYB), trade compliance verification, and AML/KYT checks
  • Risk assessment, whitelist enforcement, and fraud prevention
  • Providing customer support and regulatory reporting

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with licensed banking partners, clearing institutions, and regulatory authorities as required to deliver services or comply with applicable laws.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption, access controls, network segmentation, and regular security audits. Transaction and compliance records are retained for a minimum of 7 years or as mandated by financial regulations.

  • Email: privacy@aurellix.com

Aurellix Pay（以下简称“我们”）致力于保护您的隐私并确保您个人与企业信息的安全。本隐私政策说明我们在提供全球收付款、多币种换汇、法币⇄稳定币承兑及聚合收单服务时如何收集、使用与保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 身份与企业认证 (KYB) 信息

  • 身份信息：姓名、出生日期、国籍、政府签发的证件号码
  • 联系信息：电子邮箱、电话号码、邮寄或经营地址
  • 财务信息：银行账户详情、虚拟收款户及交易结算记录
  • 企业信息：公司注册证书、股权架构图、最终受益人 (UBO) 及跨境贸易/物流佐证单据

1.2 技术信息

  • 设备信息：IP 地址、浏览器类型、操作系统、设备标识符
  • 使用数据：访问页面、功能使用、交易时间戳与 API 调用日志

2. 信息的使用方式

  • 处理并清算全球收款、外币实时换汇、稳定币承兑与全球付款交易
  • 执行身份与企业认证（KYC/KYB）、真实贸易背景核验及反洗钱/KYT 合规筛查
  • 白名单风控校验、欺诈防范与客户支持

3. 信息共享与披露

我们不会出售您的信息，仅在交付清算服务所需范围内与持牌合作银行及清算机构共享，或在法律法规及监管要求时依法披露。

4. 数据安全与保留

我们采用传输与存储加密、权限隔离、网络分段和定期安全审计措施保护数据安全；根据金融监管要求，交易与合规档案通常至少保留 7 年。
